Lighting installation in Caledonia.

If a Caledonia kitchen now doubles as a homework table or home office, it may need better task lighting. Under-cabinet lights raise questions about where power comes from and how they are switched.

Plan the light and the work that makes it possible

A lighting project may involve fixture replacement, new wiring points or a different control layout. The contractor needs to assess existing wiring, mounting support, equipment suitability and access. Fixture appearance alone does not establish compatibility with a dimmer or the suitability of a location. Share product information and the intended room use before installation is priced.

Prepare the scope for your Caledonia property

Sketch the kitchen in words, noting where people now work, which counters need light and where the cabinets end. Collect the product sheets for any fixtures or strips you have already chosen.

Ask the contractor how the under-cabinet lights would be powered, whether the existing wiring and switch locations are suitable, and whether a dimmer suits the products you chose. Ask what finishing work the wall or ceiling would need.

What should the assessment establish?

What will power and switch the task lights in a kitchen now used in a new way? The scope answers with the switches, the dimming arrangement and any new wiring points, plus what must be opened or patched to reach them. Can the existing wiring and ceiling of the Caledonia kitchen support the chosen fixtures? The contractor's judgement on that is written down. Do the lights share a circuit with receptacles that run appliances? The report gives a yes or no. The fixtures the plan assumes are named too, so a later change of product is priced against the same description.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Do under-cabinet strips always need cable run inside the wall?

Sometimes, depending on how the lights are powered and where the cable can run. Some products plug in, others need a new connection, so the contractor should explain the route and any patching before work is priced.

How do I choose lighting for a kitchen that is also a workspace?

Start from the tasks: where you read, chop or use a laptop. Share product sheets with the contractor, who can say whether the wiring and controls suit the fixtures and whether dimming is compatible.
